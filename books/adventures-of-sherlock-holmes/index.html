About The Adventures of Sherlock Holmes

Sherlock Holmes and Dr. Watson investigate a range of puzzling cases, using close observation, careful reasoning and attention to details other people dismiss. This collection includes some of the best-known stories in the detective’s early career.

The stories are satisfying both as mysteries and as demonstrations of how a reader can reconsider evidence. Holmes’s confidence is part of the entertainment, but Watson’s narration keeps the cases grounded in friendship, surprise and the limits of what first appears obvious.
