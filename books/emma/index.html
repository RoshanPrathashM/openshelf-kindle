About Emma

Emma Woodhouse, clever and comfortably situated, enjoys matchmaking but repeatedly misreads the motives and feelings of people around her. As her assumptions collide with reality, she must learn humility and see her friends more clearly.

Austen’s comedy depends on the gap between what Emma thinks she understands and what the reader gradually notices. The novel offers a lively portrait of village society, friendship, class and the sometimes comic work of self-correction.
