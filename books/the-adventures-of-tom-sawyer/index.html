About the book

Tom Sawyer’s pranks, friendships and adventures unfold in a small town beside the Mississippi River. From schoolyard tricks to frightening discoveries, his mischievous imagination repeatedly runs up against adult expectations and real consequences.

Tom’s adventures move between school, family life, the river and frightening mysteries. The story combines comic mischief with the consequences of choices, offering both a window into nineteenth-century childhood and a narrative that has inspired many adaptations.
