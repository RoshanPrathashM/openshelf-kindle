About Frankenstein; or, The Modern Prometheus

Victor Frankenstein pursues the secret of creating life, but he is unprepared for the responsibilities that follow. Told through layered accounts, the novel explores ambition, loneliness, the need for recognition and the consequences of abandoning a creation.

Shelley’s story is more than a monster tale: it asks who is responsible when scientific curiosity outpaces care. Readers interested in early science fiction, Gothic atmosphere, ethics and the difference between appearance and character will find plenty to discuss.
