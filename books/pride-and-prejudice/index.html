About Pride and Prejudice

Elizabeth Bennet’s sharp judgement meets Fitzwilliam Darcy’s guarded pride in a comedy of manners shaped by family expectations, wealth, reputation and marriage. As misunderstandings accumulate, both characters must question the first impressions they trust.

Austen combines quick dialogue with precise social observation. The novel’s humour comes from how confidently people misunderstand one another, while its emotional movement depends on self-knowledge rather than a sudden change in fortune.
