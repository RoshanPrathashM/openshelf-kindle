About Dracula

A series of diaries, letters and recorded observations follows Jonathan Harker, Mina Murray, Dr. Seward and their allies as they confront Count Dracula. The story moves from the Carpathian Mountains to England, building dread through documents, uncertainty and pursuit.

Stoker’s epistolary structure lets the reader piece together evidence alongside the characters. The book shaped many conventions of vampire fiction, while its themes include fear of the unknown, trust, technology and collective courage.
