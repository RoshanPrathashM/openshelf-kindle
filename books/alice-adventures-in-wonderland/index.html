About Alice’s Adventures in Wonderland

Alice follows a hurried White Rabbit down a rabbit-hole and enters a world where size, time, language and ordinary rules keep changing. She meets a cast of unforgettable characters, from the Cheshire Cat to the Queen of Hearts, in a story that mixes dream logic with playful word games.

Carroll turns childhood curiosity into a literary adventure: riddles have no stable answers, authority often looks absurd, and conversations change direction without warning. The book rewards readers who enjoy imaginative worlds, nonsense, satire and illustrations that are closely tied to the text.
