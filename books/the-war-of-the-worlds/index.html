About The War of the Worlds

A sudden Martian invasion throws ordinary life in England into chaos. The narrator witnesses the collapse of familiar institutions and the limits of human power when confronted with an adversary whose technology appears almost unstoppable.

Wells uses the invasion story to unsettle assumptions about humanity’s place in the world. The novel blends suspense with a reversal of imperial certainty, asking readers to imagine how fragile civilisation can look from the outside.
