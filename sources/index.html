Open-Book Sources and Free Reading Collections | OpenShelf

Find legitimate public-domain books, openly licensed textbooks, open-access academic books and Tamil literature through trusted source collections.

OpenShelf links readers to source-hosted works and exposes live search where supported. Free access, public-domain status and permission to redistribute are different things. Always check the individual title’s licence and any regional restrictions.

Project Gutenberg ↗Discover many free ebooks and classic editions. Verify territorial status before redistribution.
Standard Ebooks ↗Carefully formatted public-domain ebooks with reading and download formats.
Open Textbook Library ↗Openly licensed textbooks with portable files and book-level licence details.
OpenStax ↗Free college and high-school textbooks with publisher-specific licences and attribution terms.
DOAB ↗Directory of peer-reviewed open-access scholarly books; check each publisher record.
Open Library ↗Discover book records and digitised editions, noting the access conditions for each item.
Tamil Wikisource ↗Tamil texts whose page-level copyright and licence information should be respected.
Project Madurai ↗Free electronic editions of Tamil literary works, distributed subject to project terms and title-specific rights.
