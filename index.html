Made for your e-reader

Get a book onto your Kindle

For Kindle 10th Generation, use your laptop or phone for downloads, then sync or transfer.

Option 1 — Send to Kindle

  1. Open a book's official source and download a supported file, preferably EPUB for normal reflowable text when available.
  2. Open Amazon Send to Kindle on your laptop or phone and sign in to the same account as your Kindle.
  3. Upload the file and wait for Amazon to process it. Review conversion or compatibility warnings.
  4. Connect your Kindle to Wi-Fi, sync, and open the book from your Library.

Option 2 — USB transfer

  1. Use a format supported by your exact Kindle. EPUB should normally be sent through Send to Kindle rather than copied directly by USB.
  2. Connect the Kindle using a data-capable USB cable.
  3. Copy a compatible Kindle file (for example, AZW3 when provided) into the device's documents folder.
  4. Eject safely, disconnect, and check your Library.

Tip: scanned PDFs with technical diagrams may be easier to read on your laptop.

What comes next

This is a discovery directory, not yet a file-hosting or upload platform.

01 · Curate

Add title-specific source links, languages, formats, and licence evidence.

02 · Verify quality

Add EPUB structure checks, metadata checks, OCR notes, accessibility checks, and device testing.

03 · Invite contributors

Build moderated submissions, rights declarations, secure file quarantine, and takedown handling before hosting files.

A community project exploring a better way to discover high-quality, legally shareable books for e-readers.

Independent project; not affiliated with Amazon, Kindle, Project Gutenberg, Standard Ebooks, OpenStax, or Open Library.
