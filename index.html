Inspect an EPUB on your laptop without uploading it. This is a quick quality check, not a replacement for full EPUBCheck validation.

EPUB health check & metadata repair

Choose an EPUB file. The browser inspects its package, metadata, reading order, table-of-contents hints, and common accessibility issues. Then you can patch title/author/language metadata and download a rebuilt copy.

Your file stays in your browser; it is not sent to OpenShelf.

Plain-text cleanup

For TXT notes or text you have permission to edit, normalize line endings and whitespace. This does not restore missing text or fix OCR by itself.

Your Kindle 10th Generation

Get a book onto your Kindle

OpenShelf is best for discovery, notes and preparation on a laptop; use Send to Kindle for compatible EPUB delivery.

Send to Kindle

  1. Find a lawful book source and download the supported file.
  2. Open Amazon Send to Kindle and sign in to the account registered on your Kindle.
  3. Upload the EPUB file and wait for the service to process it.
  4. Connect the Kindle to Wi-Fi, sync your library, and open the book.

EPUB is normally sent using Send to Kindle; it is not generally copied directly by USB as an EPUB file.

Check a book's quality

  1. Use the Book Studio to inspect an EPUB you downloaded lawfully.
  2. Check title, author, language, reading order, images, and table-of-contents signals.
  3. Repair common metadata only if the title, author, and language you enter are correct.
  4. Run EPUBCheck for full format validation and test the exported copy before relying on it.

Scanned PDFs with diagrams may be easier to read on your laptop than on the Kindle's small screen.

Honest product status

What this version can—and can't—do

OpenShelf v0.2 is a richer static prototype. It has no backend yet.

✅ Now

Live catalogue search, in-page reader panel for supported Gutenberg editions, notes, saved books, translation endpoint settings, and local EPUB inspection/metadata repair.

🔜 Next engineering milestone

Use a secure backend for author uploads, AI translation providers, EPUBCheck jobs, OCR and book conversion. Add moderation, malware screening and rights verification before hosting files.

🔒 Not included yet

OpenShelf does not host a worldwide book archive, provide a built-in paid AI model, sync notes between devices, or automatically deliver files to Kindle accounts.

Suggest an improvement or report a rights concern through the project's GitHub Issues.
